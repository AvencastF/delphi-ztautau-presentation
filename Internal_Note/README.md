## Caveats baked into the slides

- Luminosities are the run-quality good-cassette values from user-supplied DELPHI tables; not certified. Data/MC 0.982 for all years; 1994 and 1995 remain 3.1 % and 2.3 % low (open, MC side suspected).
- Detector descriptions v92e / v93d / v95d are not yet validated per year (v94c tested); neutral-shower variables are mismodelled in 1992 and 1995.
- ML numbers are closure on the hold half of the simulation; data/MC of the ML selection shows open differences (1994 ρ channels 0.94, 1992 ππ 1.24, 1995 ee 0.85); owner checkpoint acceptance pending. The 1994-only classifier column is biased (evaluated on its training rows).
- Spin fit: simulation only, Asimov, statistics only; witness significances are extrapolated beyond the toy range (≈3.7σ); reference C_kk ≈ 1.03 > 1.
- Photon calibration (2026-11-03): no calibration constant is adopted. Calibration 1 (ρ study) takes the scale from electrons, not photons, derives the migration weights from the same ρ legs it validates on, and uses ML regions about 80 % pure; its P_τ check fails in 1994 (2.7σ). Calibration 2 (l+l-γ) uses the same events for every number except the eeγ ↔ μμγ swap (2026-11-03b: the before/after closure is same-sample; the swap is the only independent test, pulls ≤ 1.7), is not usable below 5 GeV, has data 1.2–1.5× wider than simulation (not propagated, not corrected by k), and its GaussExp fit is poor on simulation. After scaling, data/simulation is 0.991–1.014 per year (1993 1.4 %, 1.1σ), not "within 1 %" in every year. The pipeline χ² of the E/E_pred histograms is dominated by low-count bins (badges are diagnostics).
