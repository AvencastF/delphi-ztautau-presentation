# Backup · cut-based decay identification

<div class="st-sub">Applied to the leading track and the neutral objects of each hemisphere, identically in every year. Both sides identified <LaTeX formula="\to" /> one of 16 charge-ordered decay pairs.</div>

<div class="st-cols st-cols-3">
<div class="kv sel"><h3>Electron</h3><dl><dt>Tag</dt><dd>DELPHI electron tag <LaTeX formula="\mathrm{ELID}\ge3" /></dd><dt>Conflict</dt><dd>two positive lepton tags <LaTeX formula="\to" /> not assigned</dd></dl></div>
<div class="kv sel"><h3>Muon</h3><dl><dt>Tag</dt><dd>DELPHI muon tag <LaTeX formula="\mathrm{MUID}\ge7" /></dd><dt>Momentum</dt><dd>no threshold</dd></dl></div>
<div class="kv sel"><h3>Charged hadron</h3><dl><dt>Veto</dt><dd>no lepton tag</dd><dt>Momentum</dt><dd><LaTeX formula="p\gt 0.05\,E_\mathrm{beam}" /></dd><dt>Confirm</dt><dd>calorimeter <LaTeX formula="E/p\lt 0.6" />, or hadron-calorimeter profile <LaTeX formula="\ge 3\,\mathrm{GeV}" /> per layer</dd></dl></div>
<div class="kv sel"><h3>Photon</h3><dl><dt>Object</dt><dd>neutral shower in the barrel electromagnetic calorimeter</dd><dt>Energy</dt><dd><LaTeX formula="E\gt 0.5\,\mathrm{GeV}" /></dd><dt>Nearby</dt><dd><LaTeX formula="\Delta R\lt 0.3" /> to the leading track</dd></dl></div>
