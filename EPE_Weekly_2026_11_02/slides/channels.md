<ul class="tk-points">
<li v-click="1"><b>Why it matters</b> The <LaTeX formula="\pi\pi" /> channel is the ideal spin analyser (<LaTeX formula="\kappa=1" /> on both sides), yet only one selected event in six is a true <LaTeX formula="\pi\pi" /> pair.</li>
<li v-click="1" class="warn"><b>Cause</b> A <LaTeX formula="\rho\to\pi\pi^0" /> whose photons are missed looks like a charged pion. Lepton channels are clean; hadron channels are not.</li>
<li v-click="2" class="ml"><b>And the direction</b> Even with the decay known, the <LaTeX formula="\tau" /> flight direction — the spin axis — is ambiguous from kinematics alone.</li>
